## Technical
The price floor in Flowdesk is not a prompt instruction, it is a CHECK
constraint plus a router gate the model cannot talk its way past. Two bookings
cannot share a slot for the same reason: a Postgres EXCLUDE constraint over a
tsrange, so a double-booking is not caught, it is impossible.

## For a Ghanaian audience
"Last price?" is how retail actually works here, so Flowdesk haggles. It just
cannot go below the floor you set. Every order out of WhatsApp, priced from your
own catalogue, on your board before you open.
