# Service operations contract

## Services and calendars

Service operations extend the existing `Service` and `Appointment` models. The managed path accepts personal appointments, professional consultations, configured repair stages and staff-assigned services. An owner or manager configures local weekly windows, exceptions, eligibility, capacity, preparation and cleanup, booking and change notice, quantity, instructions, approval, pricing and deposit terms. Each equipment group requires one available alternative. An assignee must have an active staff record and eligible staff resource in the tenant. Off-site work requires an owner or manager to attest the customer's supplied location against the configured service area. Travel estimation and courier dispatch are outside this path.

The server validates IANA local hours and closures using the instants of every reserved minute. The response contains a UTC instant alongside the local label. A skipped daylight-saving interval yields no nonexistent local start. The two real instants in a repeated hour can be selected independently. Intervals that span local midnight or leave the configured opening period are rejected. Quote results carry the settings and service revisions, authoritative total and deposit, and quantity-specific duration. A reservation rechecks the current facts under the lock.

## Lead, booking and care

The tenant workbench uses `GET /api/v1/service-operations`. The same prefix covers `PUT /settings`, `POST /resources` and `PUT /resources/:id`, `POST /catalogue` and `PUT /catalogue/:id`, `GET /slots`, `POST /leads`, `PATCH /leads/:id`, `POST /visits` and `POST /visits/:id/actions`.

When the existing workflow policy uses the Appointment or Professional Services preset and managed service is enabled, signed inbound text receipts pin new service activity to workflow version 4. Existing versions keep their event rules. Direct staff-created leads and bookings also create version 4 evidence. `SERVICE <service name> | <requirements>` captures a request with current qualification prompts. `TIMES <lead UUID>` after qualification lists current offered times. `BOOK <lead UUID> <ISO offset instant> <service revision> <settings revision>` converts the exact scoped lead. A continuation has its own durable receipt and references the original lead workflow. Failed booking requests enter review. Unsupported inquiries can be qualified by the team.

`STATUS <appointment reference>` and `PAYMENT <appointment reference>` use the current customer's exact appointment. `CANCEL`, `RESCHEDULE`, `COMPLAINT`, `MISSED`, `QUALITY` and `STAFF` route to the shared care case and persist scoped evidence. Team assignment, escalation and closure use the existing case workflow. A customer's `RESOLVED <case UUID>` must come from that case's authenticated relationship before it can serve as closure evidence. These commands do not execute a reschedule, refund, discount or work by a third party.

A visit is `HELD` when approval or payment is outstanding. `CONFIRMED` allows acknowledgment and check-in. The assigned worker can start after check-in, record configured stages, record a block or resume with evidence, and complete with handoff evidence. The manager records no-show after the configured grace, with evidence of absence. A manager can cancel eligible work with a reason. Cancellation and expiry release the appointment lane and resource claims. Rescheduling rechecks capacity and working hours, preserves captured terms and the exact invoice, and advances the notice revision. Reassignment changes authorization in the same transaction as its resource claims.

## Money and communication

A priced visit creates an existing Batch 3 invoice for precisely the tenant and customer. The payment service calculates fixed, percentage and full deposits using Prisma Decimal. A hold offers only the outstanding verified deposit. Pay-after-service opens checkout after evidenced completion. A captured deposit stays with the same invoice when the appointment time changes. Owner or manager checkout uses the existing Paystack request, independent provider verification, receipts and adjustments. A provider payment that meets the existing request rules can settle after the slot hold expires. The original appointment stays expired or cancelled. The owner reviews the exact credit. Provider verification after the payment request's own allowable window follows G1 review and leaves the independently verified evidence available. External refunds require a separate authorized process.

The existing outbox handles pending-hold details, appointment confirmation, upcoming and deposit reminders, reschedules, cancellations, no-shows, completion and authorized follow-up. Assignment notifications appear in the assigned dashboard. The notice identity combines the visit, kind and schedule revision. Current tenant, recipient, state, financial facts, consent and approved template are checked during outbox authorization and before transport. Transactional text requires the active WhatsApp window. Upcoming and no-show follow-up require `START SERVICE UPDATES`, with `STOP SERVICE UPDATES` applied from the authenticated receipt. Deposit reminders honor payment-reminder consent. Outside-window utility messaging requires recent approval for the configured exact body in the WABA and sending number. Repeat-service invitations require owner action, enabled follow-up, `START SERVICE FOLLOWUPS` and an active conversation window. Equal-timestamp opt-outs win. Provider acceptance and signed delivery are separate.

## Security and transaction boundaries

- Tenant scope originates in the authenticated actor or signed WhatsApp phone binding. Customer message proof comes from the committed G1 receipt. Legacy automatic commitment code does not process text admitted to the managed workflow.
- Managed mutations lock the current actor permission, when applicable, then the customer, then the tenant calendar. A transaction-local setting permits trusted writes to managed catalogue and appointment rows. SQL relationship guards and exclusion constraints defend against inconsistent identities and overlapping resource claims.
- Operations staff see assigned visits and allowed evidence actions. That view omits invoice and price information. Existing billing surfaces enforce established tenant and payment capabilities. Delivery-only staff are excluded. The service rechecks role and assignment during mutations.
- Payment requests lock the exact financial relationship and recheck current eligibility. Expiry and late settlement preserve historical credits. Inspect ambiguous provider outcomes before issuing new requests or communications.

## Rollback and recovery

Restart the existing receipt, settlement, shared workflow and outbound workers. A committed receipt, reservation, ledger entry and outbox effect retains its identity. Redis loss is recoverable from PostgreSQL. Investigate `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `SERVICE_*` workflow reasons and notice suppression before retrying. Resolve an unconfirmed provider outcome using signed evidence and the existing reconciliation procedures.

Keep the additive schema during binary rollback. Before managed activation, verify zero version 4 workflows, visits and managed sends. Once managed activity exists, quiesce webhook ingestion and the receipt, settlement, shared workflow and outbound workers. Freeze managed checkout, service, appointment and invoice routes on the prior binary, including the legacy diary. A health-only old runtime or service suspension preserves the augmented schema. The prior worker must be prevented from consuming version 4 records or managed sends. Record pending holds, hosted sessions and outbox intents before transition. Roll forward to the current runtime and reconcile the durable records.
